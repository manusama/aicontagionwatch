Pressure

How hard inflation is squeezing AI’s borrowed money

66 / 100

High

AI data centres are increasingly built on borrowed money, including loans and bonds that circle back to the same few companies. Inflation keeps interest rates high, and high rates make that debt dearer to carry and harder to refinance. This dial blends four readings, the bond yield counting most.

  • US 10-year bond yield (25 Nov 2026 (close))~4.85%
  • US inflation, monthly (Oct 2026)+0.4% in October
  • Brent crude oil (26 Nov 2026 (intraday))~US$91.10
  • Australian inflation (Oct 2026)4.9% a year

Each reading is scored from calm to stressed, then weighted 35/20/20/25 in the order listed.
